001 — Inspired by the Origin of Life

The First Element.
The Water You Drink.

Following the Big Bang, hydrogen — the first element to exist — eventually combined with oxygen to form H₂O, the molecule that makes all life possible. Truhydrate celebrates this remarkable journey from the universe's first element to the water we drink every day.
